# Clinical Protocol: Acute Ischemic & Hemorrhagic Stroke (PROTO-STROKE-01)
Category: STROKE
Source: Emergency Stroke Triage Protocol 2026

## Clinical Presentation & Symptoms
Rapid onset focal neurological deficits evaluated via the FAST protocol:
- F: Facial droop (uneven smile, weakness on one side of face)
- A: Arm drift (unilateral motor weakness or numbness)
- S: Speech difficulty (slurred speech, aphasia, incomprehensible words)
- T: Time of symptom onset (critical for 4.5 hour intravenous thrombolysis window)
- Sudden loss of balance, vertigo, or sudden onset thunderclap headache

## Required Ambulance Tier: ALS (Advanced Life Support)
ALS ambulance with rapid blood glucose measurement to rule out hypoglycemia mimic, continuous SpO2/BP monitoring, and hospital pre-notification.

## Hospital Capabilities Required
Routing MUST prioritize a Comprehensive Stroke Center possessing a 24/7 emergency non-contrast CT scanner, stroke neurology team, and neuro-interventional thrombectomy capabilities.

# Clinical Protocol: Thermal & Chemical Burn Emergencies (PROTO-BURN-01)
Category: BURNS
Source: National Burn Center Standards & SahayAI Emergency Guidelines 2026

## Clinical Presentation & Symptoms
Thermal injuries resulting from LPG cylinder explosions, residential fires, scalding liquids, industrial flash fires, or chemical contact:
- Partial or full thickness burns involving >10% Total Body Surface Area (TBSA)
- Burns involving critical areas: face, hands, feet, genitalia, major joints
- Inhalation injury: soot in nostrils/mouth, singed facial hair, stridor, hoarseness, carbonaceous sputum
- Circumferential chest or extremity burns threatening respiration or distal perfusion

## Required Ambulance Tier: ALS (Advanced Life Support)
For burns >10% TBSA or suspected inhalation airway edema, ALS ambulance is mandatory to secure the airway before laryngeal edema closes the glottis.

## Mandatory Vehicle Equipment
1. Sterile dry burn sheets and plastic wrap dressings
2. High-flow humidified oxygen
3. Intravenous crystalloid fluids (Ringer's Lactate for Parkland formula)
4. Suction apparatus and endotracheal intubation supplies

## Hospital Capabilities Required
Patient must be routed directly to a specialized tertiary Burn Unit facility (e.g., Sassoon General Hospital, Aditya Birla Memorial Hospital).

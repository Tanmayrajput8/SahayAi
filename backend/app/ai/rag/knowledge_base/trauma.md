# Clinical Protocol: Major Physical Trauma & Road Traffic Collisions (PROTO-TRAUMA-01)
Category: TRAUMA
Source: SahayAI Trauma Response Directorate 2026

## Clinical Presentation & Symptoms
Major trauma includes high-velocity road accidents (bike collisions, highway crashes), falls from height (>10 feet), penetrating injuries, and industrial crush injuries. Symptoms:
- Uncontrolled external hemorrhage or suspected internal abdominal/thoracic bleeding
- Obvious limb deformities, compound open fractures, pelvic instability
- Traumatic head injury, skull depression, loss of consciousness, unequal pupils
- Flail chest, paradoxical breathing, severe spinal pain with sensory loss

## Required Ambulance Tier: ALS (Advanced Life Support)
Major multi-system trauma requires an ALS ambulance with trained paramedics capable of cervical spine immobilization, needle thoracostomy, and rapid vascular access.

## Mandatory Vehicle Equipment
1. Rigid Cervical Collars (pediatric and adult sizes)
2. Full spine backboard with spider straps and head blocks
3. Commercial arterial tourniquets and hemostatic gauze dressings
4. Traction splints and pelvic stabilization binder
5. Bag-valve-mask (BVM) and portable suction unit

## Hospital Capabilities Required
Patient must be routed directly to an accredited Level-1 or Level-2 Trauma Center equipped with emergency surgical theatres, trauma anesthesiologist, CT scan, and blood bank availability.

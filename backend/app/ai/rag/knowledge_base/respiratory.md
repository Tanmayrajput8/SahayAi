# Clinical Protocol: Acute Respiratory Failure & Severe Asthma (PROTO-RESP-01)
Category: RESPIRATORY
Source: Acute Pulmonary Emergency Guidelines 2026

## Clinical Presentation & Symptoms
Severe acute respiratory failure includes acute asthma exacerbation, acute pulmonary edema, COPD exacerbation, and foreign body airway obstruction:
- Inability to speak in full sentences; speaking in single words only
- Severe tachypnea (>30 breaths/min) or bradypnea (<10 breaths/min with impending arrest)
- Intercostal and sternocleidomastoid retractions, tracheal tug, paradoxical abdominal breathing
- Audible wheezing, silent chest (critical ominous sign), or stridor
- Peripheral pulse oximetry SpO2 < 88% on ambient room air

## Required Ambulance Tier: ALS (Advanced Life Support)
Requires ALS ambulance equipped with mechanical transport ventilator, continuous nebulization, and CPAP/BiPAP non-invasive ventilation mask.

## Hospital Capabilities Required
Patient must be transported to a hospital equipped with intensive care unit (ICU) and pulmonary critical care capabilities.

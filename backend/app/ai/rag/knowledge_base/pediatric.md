# Clinical Protocol: Pediatric Acute Resuscitation & Shock (PROTO-PED-01)
Category: PEDIATRIC
Source: Pediatric Advanced Life Support (PALS) Guidance 2026

## Clinical Presentation & Symptoms
Critical illness or injury in neonates, infants, or children:
- Status epilepticus / active generalized convulsions lasting >5 minutes
- Severe lethargy, unresponsiveness, poor peripheral perfusion (capillary refill >3 seconds)
- Severe dehydration with sunken eyes, depressed fontanelle, and dry mucous membranes
- High-grade fever with petechial or purpuric rash (suspected meningococcemia)

## Required Ambulance Tier: ALS (Advanced Life Support)
ALS ambulance with pediatric length-based resuscitation tape (Broselow Tape), pediatric airway blades (Miller/Macintosh 0-2), micro-drip infusion sets, and pediatric defibrillation pads.

## Hospital Capabilities Required
Emergency transport must prioritize medical centers with a 24/7 dedicated Pediatric Intensive Care Unit (PICU) and neonatologist/pediatric intensivist on call.
